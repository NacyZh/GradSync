# Feature 017 Moderated Usability Protocol

Status: pending human sessions.

## Advisor Setup

- Recruit at least ten representative advisors.
- Ask each participant to configure one milestone, one deliverable with evidence
  and reviewer, one report template, one decision, and one triaged risk without
  coaching.
- Record completion, assistance points, elapsed time, and anonymized notes.
- Pass threshold: at least 90% complete the setup unassisted.

## Handover Retrieval

- Provide a seeded historical project to at least ten representative users.
- Ask each participant to retrieve accepted evidence, a report source metric,
  the current decision and predecessor, and an active risk with revision history.
- Pass threshold: each complete retrieval takes under two minutes.

No result is recorded until moderated sessions occur; automated UI tests do not
substitute for AC-017 or AC-018 human evidence.
